## Goal

Every bucket and every opened word shows a picture of that specific word, not a
level-wide illustration.

## Resolution

- 750 images, one per locale/level/word, 256×256 WebP under `static/art/words/`.
- Generated by `scripts/generate_word_art.py` from reviewed bilingual prompts in
  `scripts/word_glosses.json` (`localized word (English gloss)`).
- Used in `Bucket.svelte`, `WordPanel.svelte` and the Word Book thumbnails.
- Missing images degrade cleanly; `src/lib/content/word-art.test.ts` requires all 750.
