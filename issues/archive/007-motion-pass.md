## Goal

Add life without turning the game into a carnival. The chosen direction is
**vivo ma calmo**.

## Work

- Bucket cards arrive in a short stagger and closed buckets float gently.
- Solving a word pops the lid, draws the check and raises the word.
- A light confetti burst plays on the word-complete card.
- Existing slot/tile pop and hint cues stay.
- All motion is disabled by the global `prefers-reduced-motion` rule.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Motion is visible but subtle on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Reduced-motion mode removes it.
- [ ] Visual snapshots updated.
